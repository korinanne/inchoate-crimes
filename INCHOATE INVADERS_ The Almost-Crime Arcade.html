INCHOATE INVADERS

MISSION BRIEFING

What you are hunting

An inchoate offense is a crime that is "only partly in existence." All three are specific-intent crimes, and a defendant can be convicted of one even though the intended crime never happened (Outline p. 38, § IV).

LEVEL 1 · SOLICITATION

Asking someone else to commit a crime, intending that they do it.

LEVELS 2-3 · CONSPIRACY

Agreeing with someone to commit a crime, and what liability follows.

LEVELS 4-5 · ATTEMPT

A substantial step toward a crime, with the specific intent to commit it.

LEVEL 6 · MERGER (BOSS)

Which of these charges survive when more than one applies.

How you play

THE CATCHER (LEVELS 1 AND 3)

A case card falls toward three verdict bins. Steer the gavel with ← and →, then let the card land, or slam it down early with ↓ or SPACE for a bigger speed bonus. Clicking a bin moves the gavel there and slams.

The verdict bins. GUILTY (or LIABLE) and NOT GUILTY (or NOT LIABLE) mean the outline gives one answer no matter which rule applies. SPLIT means the outline describes competing rules, e.g., common law versus the MPC, that reach different results on those facts.

THE SHOOTER (LEVELS 2, 5, AND 6)

Answer targets descend in a row. Move with ← and →, fire with SPACE. The first target you hit is your answer. Prefer not to aim? Press the target's letter key (A-D) or click it, and your ship lines up and fires for you.

THE LADDER (LEVEL 4)

Climb with ↑ and ↓ to the highest rung the facts reach, then lock in with SPACE or ENTER. Clicking a rung climbs there and locks in.

Scoring and lives

A correct answer earns 100 points plus a speed bonus of up to 50. Starting with your third correct answer in a row, each one adds 25 more. A wrong answer or a timeout costs a life. You start with 3 lives and can hold up to 6: a perfect level earns a life and 250 points, and 5 correct in a row within a level earns a life once per level. Lose them all and the game ends.

After every answer the explanation stays on screen until you press NEXT. Nothing you need to read is ever on a clock.

Reading speed

Every round starts only when you say so. Pick how fast the clocks run; you can change this anytime from the ⏱ button at the top, even in the middle of a question.
